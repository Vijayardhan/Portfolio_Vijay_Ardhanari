EchoGlove: Assistive Smart Glove for Gesture-Based Communication and Health Monitoring
DOI: 10.22214/ijraset.2025.66821
Developed an ESP32-based assistive glove that recognises hand gestures and converts them into speech. The system also supports heart-rate monitoring, obstacle detection, Bluetooth communication, and audio output to improve communication and safety for people with speech impairments.

Automated Drug Dispenser: Revolutionizing Healthcare Through Advanced Pharmacy Management
Paper ID: ICCET250787
Developed an automated pharmacy system that digitises prescriptions using QR codes containing doctor, patient, medication, and dosage information. The dispenser separates medicines into morning, afternoon, and night doses, supports symptom-based OTC purchases, and integrates digital payments to reduce dispensing errors and improve efficiency.
Presented at the ICCET conference and submitted for publication in a Scopus-indexed journal.
